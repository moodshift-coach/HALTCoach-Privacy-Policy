Who We Are

HALT Coach ("we", "our") is an iOS application designed to support people who have completed addiction recovery programs. The app provides AI-powered coaching conversations to help users maintain their wellbeing after formal treatment.

What Data We Collect

Data We Collect — None

We, as the developer, collect no personal data whatsoever. There are no user accounts, no registration, no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising identifiers, and no tracking of any kind.

Data Stored Locally on Your Device

The following data is stored exclusively on your iPhone and never transmitted to our servers (we have none):

  • Coaching session conversations and messages
  • HALT check-in results and history
  • Streak and session counters
  • AI memory summaries (if feature is enabled)
  • App preferences and settings

Data Processed by AI (Not Stored by Us)

Your conversation messages are sent to Anthropic's Claude API to generate AI responses in real time. This data is processed by Anthropic under their privacy policy. We do not store, access, or log these conversations on any server.

→ Anthropic Privacy Policy: anthropic.com/privacy

Subscription Plans & Data

FREE PLAN

PREMIUM · $29.99/month

Full Access

HALT Coach contains no analytics SDKs, no advertising frameworks, no crash reporting tools, and no social media integrations. The only external connection the app makes is to the Anthropic API for AI responses and to Apple's servers for subscription management.

Welche Daten Wir Erheben

Erhobene Daten — Keine

Wir als Entwickler erheben keinerlei personenbezogene Daten. Es gibt keine Benutzerkonten, keine Registrierung, keine Analysen, keine Absturzberichte, keine Werbe-IDs und keinerlei Tracking.

  • Streak- und Sitzungszähler

Von KI Verarbeitete Daten (Nicht Von Uns)

Deine Gesprächsnachrichten werden an die Claude API von Anthropic gesendet, um KI-Antworten in Echtzeit zu generieren. Diese Daten werden von Anthropic gemäß ihrer Datenschutzrichtlinie verarbeitet. Wir speichern, greifen nicht auf diese Gespräche zu und protokollieren sie nicht auf einem Server.
